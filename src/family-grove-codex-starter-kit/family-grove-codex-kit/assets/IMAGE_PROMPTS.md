# Original raster asset generation prompts

The five PNG files were created with the built-in image-generation workflow using the following visual briefs. They share a soft, premium 3D-storybook/clay look. They are flat 2D raster images, not mesh files.

## Hero family tree

> Wide website hero: cinematic giant flourishing ancestral tree with mint, turquoise, coral and gold foliage, empty oval gold portrait frames hanging from branches, misty soft mint negative space on the left, peach sunrise, playful meadow, rich but gentle 3D clay-storybook look. No people, text, words or watermark.

## Family Grove logo

> Transparent isolated premium 3D-clay emblem: a family tree with five colorful round leaf clusters and a central interlocking heart-shaped teal trunk; instantly recognizable at small icon size. No words, branding text or background.

## Empty sapling

> Transparent isolated miniature 3D-clay seedling with three leaves, a tiny flower, and two blank small portrait frames. Soft mint/jade, cheerful gentle beginner experience. No people or words.

## Linked family trees

> Transparent isolated two miniature 3D-clay trees, one jade and one apricot, linked by a graceful golden bridge of leaves. Separate roots, empty portrait medallions, safe interconnectedness, no people or words.

## Occasion celebration

> Wide festive storybook 3D-clay background: central distant family tree, bunting, lanterns, balloons, presents, flowers and sparkles, inclusive celebration without a specific religious symbol. Uncluttered space for overlay text, mint/apricot/lavender/gold, no text or people.

Use the original PNGs supplied in this kit. Regeneration is optional and should preserve their intended roles.
